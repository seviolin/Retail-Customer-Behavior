# Retail Customer Behavior
An end-to-end retail data analysis project exploring customer behavior and sales performance through EDA using Python, KPI analysis using SQL, and interactive dashboard development using Tableau.

1. Introduction
2. Overview Dashboard

### 1. Introduction
The project aims to analyze the shopping behavior dataset to identify sales performance, customer behavior, and analyze product performance, providing direction for business planning.

### 3. Dataset
- Rows: 9,900
- Columns: 21
- Key features:
  - Customer demographics (name, segment, country, city, state, postal code, region)
  - Purchase detail (order id, shipping mode, category, subcategory, product name, sales, quantity, discount, profit)
 
### 4. SQL Queries
The SQL analysis consists of 10 types of queries, covering key performance indicators (KPIs), revenue comparisons, and sales performance:

1. **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Calculate the overall retail business performance.
2. **Revenue Comparison:** Compare total revenue across regions, cities, product categories, shipping types, and customer segments.
3. **Top 5 Products by Revenue:** Identify the five products generating the highest total revenue.
4. **Annual Revenue Performance:** Identify the year with the highest and lowest total revenue.
5. **Monthly Revenue Analysis (2018):** Determine which month recorded the highest and lowest revenue in 2018.
6. **Top and Bottom 5 Products (November 2018):** Identify the five highest-selling and lowest-selling products during November 2018.
